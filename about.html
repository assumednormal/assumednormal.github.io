About

Statistician by training. Builder by habit.

I’ve spent 14 years inside organizations that needed practical results more than perfect architecture: metrics leadership could trust, models that moved revenue, and clear calls on what was worth building next.

I’ve been the first data scientist at a fast-growing infrastructure company, run the data science and analytics function at a venture-backed real estate company, built credit models for consumer lending marketplaces, and today lead data science at a YC-backed AI agent platform. Along the way I’ve used AI and machine learning not as buzzwords but as working tools, most recently to evaluate AI voice agents and to ship internal tools in a fraction of the time.

Through assumednormal, I bring that same experience to small and medium-sized businesses. Most already have the data they need. They need someone senior to make it reliable, turn it into decisions, and show them where AI genuinely helps.
