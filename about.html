About

I’m Max. I’ve spent roughly 14 years working across data analysis, data science / machine learning, and data / analytics engineering.

Most of that time has been inside organizations that needed practical results more than perfect architectures: getting numbers that leadership could trust, building the pipelines and models that actually got used, and helping teams decide what was worth doing next.

I’m now focused on helping local small businesses get the same kind of clarity without having to hire a full-time data person. The businesses I work with usually already have the data — they just don’t have a reliable way to turn it into decisions.

How I work

I prefer a simple structure: a monthly advisory retainer for ongoing direction, prioritization, and review, with hands-on project work scoped separately when something needs to be built. This keeps the relationship focused on outcomes rather than hours.

I work primarily with operational and financial data. For professional services clients (including law firms), that means things like time, billing, costs, and marketing source data — not privileged matter content.

Background

My experience spans analysis, modeling, and the engineering needed to keep data reliable. I’m comfortable moving between defining the right metrics, cleaning up conflicting reports, building forecasting or segmentation models, and setting up the pipelines that make the numbers stay trustworthy.

Outside of work I live in the Austin area with my wife and two dogs. I still enjoy scraping and analyzing data for its own sake.
