About

Max Mitchell

Data Advisor

Austin area · ~14 years across analytics, data science, and data engineering

I’ve spent the last 14 years inside organizations that needed practical results more than perfect architectures: building metrics leadership could trust, delivering machine learning models that actually drove revenue, and helping teams decide what was worth tackling next.

Today, I help local small and mid-size businesses get that same institutional clarity without the heavy cost of a full-time data team. Most businesses already produce the numbers they need; they just need a reliable way to synthesize them into confident decisions.

Background & Experience

Prior to advising independent businesses, I led data science and analytics efforts across credit risk, sports analytics, real estate pricing, and cloud infrastructure:

  • Veritus — Head of Data Science
  • Upstart — Staff Data Analyst
  • Divvy Homes — Principal Data Scientist / Head of Data Science & Analytics
  • Marcus by Goldman Sachs — Associate Data Scientist
  • MLB Advanced Media — Senior Data Scientist
  • Cloudflare — Data Scientist (first data science hire)
  • TransUnion — Senior Statistical Analyst
  • U.S. Census Bureau — Mathematical Statistician

I hold an M.S. in Statistical Practice and a B.S. in Mathematics/Statistics from Carnegie Mellon University.

How I Work

I favor a transparent model: a monthly advisory retainer for ongoing prioritization, review, and senior judgment, combined with hands-on project work scoped separately when systems need to be built. This structure keeps incentives aligned on outcomes rather than billable hours.

For professional services clients (such as law firms or accounting practices), I work primarily with operational and financial data (billing efficiency, margin, marketing channel ROI, staff utilization) — never privileged client matter content.

Personal

I live in the Austin area with my wife and two dogs. Outside of work, I still enjoy web scraping and analyzing odd datasets for fun.
