Contact

A short conversation is usually the quickest way to see if there's a fit.

Email directly:

max@assumednormal.com

You can also connect on LinkedIn.

When reaching out, it’s helpful to briefly outline:

  • The type of business you operate
  • The key decision, forecasting challenge, or manual reporting pain you are looking to address
  • The primary software or tools you currently use (e.g., QuickBooks, Salesforce, Google Sheets, custom DB)

I typically respond within one to two business days. If our goals align, we will schedule a 20-minute call to discuss whether a diagnostic audit, monthly retainer, or a targeted project is the right step.

I am based in the Austin area and prioritize working with local businesses.
