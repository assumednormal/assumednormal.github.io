Contact

A short conversation is usually the best way to start.

Email me at max@assumednormal.com.

In your message, it helps if you include:

  • What kind of business you run
  • The decision or problem you’re trying to solve (or the reporting pain you’re dealing with)
  • Roughly how large the business is and what tools you currently use for data

I typically reply within a couple of business days. If it looks like a good fit, we can schedule a short call to discuss whether an advisory retainer, a diagnostic, or a focused project makes the most sense.

I’m based in the Austin area and primarily work with local businesses.
