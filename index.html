The AI on-ramp

A practical path to AI for small and medium businesses.

Most AI projects stall because the data underneath isn’t ready, or nobody picked a problem worth solving. I start with your data and your workflows, pilot one high-value use case, measure it, then scale what works.

  • Automate the weekly report someone builds by hand
  • Sort and route inbound emails, tickets, and requests
  • Pull structured data out of PDFs, invoices, and forms
  • Let your team ask questions of clean, governed data
  • Get your staff productive with AI tools, safely

  1. Assess

    Audit your data, tools, and recurring work. Deliver a ranked map of where AI and automation pay off, and what has to be fixed first.

  2. Pilot

    Build one use case end to end with a clear baseline, so you know whether it saved hours or dollars before spending more.

  3. Roll out

    Harden what worked: reliable data pipelines, simple guardrails, human review where it matters, and training for your team.

  4. Operate

    Ongoing advisory to monitor quality, tune models, and choose the next opportunity as tools change.
