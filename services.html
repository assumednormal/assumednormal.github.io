Services

Senior advisory judgment plus hands-on execution when you need something built.

The Engagement Model

Most partnerships begin with a monthly advisory retainer. This gives leadership continuous access to senior judgment: defining core KPIs, reviewing financial models or dashboards, prioritizing technical investments, and identifying high-leverage opportunities.

When you need concrete infrastructure built — an automated pipeline, an executive dashboard, a customer forecasting model, or database cleanup — we scope it as an independent project at a fixed rate. This protects the retainer for strategy and prevents open-ended scope creep.

We often start with a short diagnostic audit of your existing spreadsheets, software, and reports so we can agree on the highest-value priorities before committing to ongoing work.
