Engagement model

How we work together

  1. Diagnostic

    A short, fixed-fee review of your spreadsheets, software, reports, and recurring manual work. You get a written assessment and a ranked list of opportunities, including where AI fits and where it doesn’t yet.

  2. Advisory retainer

    Ongoing access to senior judgment: defining KPIs, reviewing dashboards and models, choosing tools and vendors, and prioritizing what to build next. Monthly, cancel anytime.

  3. Scoped projects

    When you need something built, such as a pipeline, a dashboard, a forecasting model, or an AI workflow, we scope it separately at a fixed price. That keeps the retainer for strategy and avoids open-ended scope creep.
