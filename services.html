Services

Ongoing advice plus hands-on work when you need it.

The model

Most clients start with a monthly advisory retainer. This gives you regular access to senior judgment: prioritization, metric definitions, review of reports or models, and guidance on what to do next.

When you need something built — a dashboard, a pipeline, a forecasting model, a data quality cleanup — we scope that as a separate project at a contracted rate. This keeps the retainer focused on direction and prevents it from turning into open-ended implementation.

Many engagements begin with a short diagnostic so we can see the current state and agree on the highest-value next steps before committing to ongoing work.

Data & Analytics Engineering

  • Making reporting reliable without constant manual work

Who this is for

Local small and mid-size businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets but don’t need (or can’t justify) a full-time data hire. Common fits include:

  • Law firms and other professional services
  • Accounting and bookkeeping practices
  • Medical, dental, and specialty practices
  • Specialized trades and home services
  • Other local businesses with real operational or financial data complexity
