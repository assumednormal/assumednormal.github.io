01Summary

Client Company wants to reduce the time leadership spends assembling weekly reports and to understand where AI could take recurring work off the team’s plate. This proposal covers a three-week diagnostic that ends with a written assessment and a ranked roadmap.

Outcome A clear picture of your data today, the three highest-value opportunities, and a recommended first pilot with expected effort and payoff.

02Scope

  • Interviews with leadership and two to three operators who own recurring reporting
  • Review of data sources: accounting, CRM, scheduling, and shared spreadsheets
  • Assessment of data quality, access, and readiness for automation and AI
  • Ranked opportunity map with effort, expected impact, and risks
